# Sources and citation guide

All sources were accessed on **14 November 2026**.

## How to cite

For each source give the title, the organization (NASA Human Research Program or Canadian Space
Agency), the URL and the access date. When a figure comes from a PDF, cite the PDF **and** the NASA
page that links to it.

- **Web page:** NASA Human Research Program. "Risk of Spaceflight-Induced Bone Changes."
  https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-spaceflight-induced-bone-changes/ (accessed 14 Nov 2026).
- **PDF:** NASA Human Research Program. "Cardiovascular Risk Evidence Book 2022."
  https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/evidence/reports/Cardio%20Risk%20EB%202022%20FINAL_4-3-2023.pdf.
  Linked from "Risk of Cardiovascular Adaptations", https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-cardiovascular-adaptations/
  (accessed 14 Nov 2026).

## Notes

- **Link to verify:** the immune-risk page and the hypoxia page both link to `risk.aspx?i=85`.
  Confirm which risk it describes before citing it.
- **70% figure:** it refers to "some swelling" at the back of the eye, not to clinically significant SANS.
- **PDF links** were copied from NASA pages. Confirm each one opens before citing it.
- **Simulated data:** every value generated by this project is labeled `SIMULATED` and must be kept
  separate from the NASA figures cited here.
